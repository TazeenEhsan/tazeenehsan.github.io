**Role: Lead Developer**

WowAddons is a WooCommerce plugin focused on advanced product customization. As Lead Developer, I designed and built the plugin's core functionality and owned its ongoing performance and UX improvements.

## What it does

- Product customization options (text fields, file uploads, dropdowns, and more)
- Product swatches and sliders
- Conditional logic between product options
- Upselling and cross-selling
- Dynamic, per-product configuration of options

## My role

- Designed and developed plugin functionality end-to-end
- Implemented complex WooCommerce features on top of the WooCommerce product and cart APIs
- Improved the customer-facing configuration experience
- Maintained and improved plugin performance
